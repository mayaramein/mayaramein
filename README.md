## About Me

I am a **results-driven Software Engineer** who designs and ships full-stack systems end-to-end — from clarifying requirements with clients to shipping secure APIs, admin panels, storefronts, and integrations.

Since **December 2022**, I have been a **.NET Software Developer at Codeware (Kuwait, remote)**, owning delivery across e-commerce, legal/ERP, charity/endowment platforms, and mobile backends in a small collaborative team.

In parallel, I invest in **data science, cloud, and generative AI** (DEBI, DEPI, ALX, and continuous learning), so I can connect solid engineering fundamentals with data-informed and AI-assisted products.

**What I care about**
- Turning business rules into maintainable software (pricing/tax, workflows, payments, permissions)
- Clean boundaries: Clean Architecture, modular monoliths, and pragmatic patterns over hype
- Client communication — estimates, trade-offs, and protecting production money/data paths
- Bilingual products (**Arabic / English**, RTL) for real markets
